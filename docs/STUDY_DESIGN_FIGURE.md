# Study design figure provenance

The diagram in `figures/manuscript/study_design_domains.svg` is a **schematic design figure**. Inputs are documented experiment domains and chronological transfer-protocol definitions in `docs/MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT.md`. It is **not** a GIS map; the drawn rectangle positions and distances are not geographically calibrated. No gridded prediction metrics are inferred from it.

For empirical spatial error and monthly seasonality figures, archived scalar JSON/CSV metrics are insufficient. Preserve this distinction and use actual valid-time/gridded predictions and corresponding ERA5 truth with defensible alignment before generating those figures.
